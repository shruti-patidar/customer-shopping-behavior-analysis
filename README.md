# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using Python, SQL, and Power BI. The objective is to identify purchasing patterns, revenue drivers, customer demographics, subscription trends, and category performance to support business decision-making.

## Tools Used

* Python
* PostgreSQL
* Power BI
* DAX
* Excel

## Business Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
* Identify high-value customer segments.
* Evaluate revenue contribution by gender, age group, and subscription status.
* Analyze category performance.
* Generate actionable business recommendations.

## Dataset Information

* Total Records: 3,900
* Customer Demographics
* Product Categories
* Purchase Amount
* Subscription Status
* Review Ratings
* Previous Purchases
* Shipping Information

## SQL Business Questions

1. Revenue generated by male vs female customers.
2. Customers using discounts while spending above average.
3. Top-rated products based on customer reviews.
4. Purchase comparison between shipping methods.
5. Spending behavior of subscribers vs non-subscribers.
6. Products with the highest discount usage.
7. Customer segmentation analysis.
8. Top-selling products by category.
9. Relationship between repeat buyers and subscriptions.
10. Revenue contribution by age group.

## Dashboard KPIs

* Total Revenue: $233K
* Total Customers: 3.9K
* Average Order Value: $59.8
* Average Rating: 3.75
* Subscribers: 1.1K

## Key Insights

* Male customers contribute 67.7% of total revenue.
* Senior customers generate the highest revenue.
* Clothing is the highest revenue-generating category.
* Non-subscribers contribute nearly 73% of total revenue.
* Clothing and Accessories are the strongest-performing categories.

## Business Recommendations

* Increase marketing efforts toward female customers.
* Expand Clothing and Accessories product offerings.
* Introduce loyalty rewards to convert non-subscribers into subscribers.
* Create targeted promotions for Adult and Young Adult customers.
* Use bundle offers and cross-selling strategies to increase average order value.
